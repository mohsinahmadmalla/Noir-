Our Story

Coffee is More Than a Drink — It's a Ritual

Founded in 2019 by two coffee-obsessed travelers, Noir & Bean was born from journeys through Ethiopia, Colombia and Indonesia. We source directly from farmers we trust, roast in-house weekly, and pour with purpose.

Our cafe is a space to slow down — to read, to connect, to taste something extraordinary.
